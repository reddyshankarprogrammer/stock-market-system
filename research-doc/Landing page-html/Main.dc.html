What beta traders say

Questions

Which brokers can I import from?+

Groww, Zerodha, Binance and Exness statements today, plus any CSV file. The AI maps unfamiliar columns for you, and you confirm before anything is saved.

Does the AI coach tell me what to buy or sell?+

No. It explains patterns in your own past trades and suggests rules for your plan. It is educational and is not investment advice.

How does the device PIN work?+

After you sign in fully once, you can set a 6-digit PIN for that device only. It locks after five wrong tries, and placing trades or moving money always asks for your password or passkey.

Is my trading data safe?+

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, kept in our own infrastructure, and deleted on request. AI features send only the trades needed to answer your question.

Can I use it on my phone?+

Yes. Open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. Native iOS and Android apps are planned.
