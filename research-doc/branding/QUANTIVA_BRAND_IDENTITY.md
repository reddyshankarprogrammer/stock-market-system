# Logo Concept

## Recommended Concept

### Quantum Q

The Quantiva logo should center around a clean geometric "Q" symbol.

The symbol should represent:

- Quantitative Trading
- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Analytics
- Market Intelligence
- Continuous Learning
- Portfolio Growth

### Visual Elements

Combine:

- Q letterform
- Upward trend line
- Candlestick patterns
- Connected data nodes
- Quantitative analytics

into a single premium geometric symbol.

---

# Design Principles

## Must Include

- Geometric Q
- Stock market inspiration
- Growth representation
- Modern fintech aesthetics
- Enterprise-grade appearance

## Must Avoid

- Robot faces
- Android characters
- Brain icons
- Cryptocurrency symbols
- Dollar symbols
- Cartoon effects
- Gaming style graphics

### Why?

Quantiva should be perceived as:

- Bloomberg-grade
- TradingView-grade
- Institutional
- Professional
- Premium

AI should be represented subtly through data structures and intelligent design rather than obvious robotic imagery.
